# Alô, Tucano Viagens!

![preview](og.png)

**Play / 在线玩 / Jogar:** https://jabarrick.github.io/tucano-viagens/

A browser game set in Brazil, 1992. You are the new phone operator at a (fictional) travel agency in São Paulo: pick up, greet in Portuguese, find out what the caller wants, and sell the right trip before their patience runs out.

1992 年的巴西，没有互联网，订旅行全靠电话。你是圣保罗一家（虚构）旅行社的新接线员：接听、用葡语问候、问清需求，在客人失去耐心之前卖出合适的行程。

Brasil, 1992. Você é o novo atendente de uma agência de viagens (fictícia) em São Paulo: atenda, cumprimente, descubra o que o cliente quer e venda a viagem certa antes que a paciência acabe.

- 33 destinations on a map of Brazil, each with seasons, weather and local activities
- Campaign (the twelve months of 1992) and Endless mode
- Prices in dollars, budgets in cruzeiros, and an exchange rate that climbs every month
- 中文 / English / Português
- One self-contained `index.html`; no build step, no dependencies

The agency, its staff and its callers are fictional. The exchange rate is an approximation of 1992 inflation, not historical data.
